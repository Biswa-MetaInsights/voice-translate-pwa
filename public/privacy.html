Privacy

This beta is designed to process speech live. The app itself does not currently use an account system or its own database to save your conversations.

What is processed

When you use the microphone, audio is sent to cloud speech services for transcription. The resulting text and up to three recent transcript phrases are sent to cloud AI services for translation and language detection. When Natural voice is selected and you request playback, translated text is also sent for cloud speech generation. Device voice uses the voices available through your device and browser.

What this app stores locally

Your theme choice, favorite languages, recent languages, language-use counts, and translation, voice and microphone preferences are stored locally in your browser or installed PWA on your device. Conversation text is held in the current page's memory. Translation timing logs contain durations and model names, not conversation text.

What this app does not currently do

  • No user account is required.
  • No conversation history is intentionally stored in an application database.
  • No precise location is required for language selection.

Visitor statistics

When traffic measurement is enabled, Vercel Web Analytics measures visits and page views on the public production website. Aggregate reports can include referring websites, country, device and browser. This integration does not send microphone audio, transcripts, translations, or URL query strings and fragments as analytics events. It does not add analytics cookies. Measurement is skipped when your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Statistics are available to authorized project members through Vercel.

Feedback

Submitting the form saves the topic, message, and any optional name or reply email you provide to the VoiceTranslator feedback database. The team can mark submissions as new, reviewing, planned, in progress, done, or declined and add internal notes. The public form can add submissions but cannot view or change stored feedback. Each record is labeled with its app name. When a submission is saved, its app name, topic, message, and any optional name or reply email are also emailed to MetaInsights BV using Resend, an email delivery provider. Resend processes this information to deliver the notification. If database submission is unavailable, the form prepares an email to MetaInsights BV as a fallback. The form does not collect microphone audio, transcripts, or translations. Please do not include private conversation audio, transcripts, or other sensitive information.

Third-party processing

Speech transcription and translation are provided through Vercel AI Gateway and underlying AI model providers. Their infrastructure may process request data according to their own service terms and data-handling policies.

Production note
