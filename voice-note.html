How to open a WhatsApp voice note
  1. In WhatsApp, press and hold the voice note and open the available Share options.
  2. On supported Android devices, choose VoiceTranslator if you have installed the web app and it appears in the Share list.
  3. If it is not listed, save the note to your files. Open this page and choose the saved audio file. On iPhone, use Save to Files where WhatsApp offers it.
  4. Select the languages, confirm processing, then tap Translate voice note.

WhatsApp’s Forward action sends to WhatsApp contacts. To open the note here, use Share or select a saved file. Availability depends on your device and WhatsApp version.

The app holds the selected note and result in memory until you clear or leave this page. It does not save them to an application database. Cloud providers apply their own processing and retention terms.
