App name: VoiceTranslator.eu

Short description: Translate speech and voice notes, with text and audio playback.

Full description:

VoiceTranslator.eu helps you understand spoken words in another language.

Use Speak for live speech translation. Use Conversation to take turns speaking with someone in another language. Choose a saved or shared voice note to read or hear a translation. Copy translated text, choose available playback voices, and export supported conversation audio.

Voice notes are processed only after you select a note and agree to processing. Audio and text are processed by cloud services; an internet connection is required. Microphone permission is needed for live speech, not to browse the app. Translation and speech recognition can make mistakes: check important names, numbers and instructions against the original.

On compatible Android devices, VoiceTranslator can receive a supported audio note through the system Share menu. You can also choose a saved audio file. This app does not access or translate personal WhatsApp conversations automatically and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

Voice-note import supports the formats and languages shown in the app, with a 4 MB input limit. Availability and voice playback depend on your device and the selected language. No account is required.
